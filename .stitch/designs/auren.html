02 — Materiality

Made to be lived with.

Every surface tells a story of where it began. We choose materials not for temporary perfection, but for their structural truth and their quiet capacity to grow warmer and richer across years of daily ritual.

01

American Walnut

Sustainably harvested dark heartwood with intricate figuring that softens into a warm amber luster over decades.

02

White Oak

Quarter-sawn for architectural stability, revealing fine medullary rays and tactile, open-pore warmth.

03

Honed Travertine

Natural sedimentary limestone from Tuscan quarries, left unpolished to preserve its organic porous voids.

04

Belgian Linen

Heavyweight long-staple flax fiber, unbleached and breathable, designed to soften gracefully with use.

05

Vegetable Leather

Cured slowly with oak bark tannins without harsh heavy metals, recording an indelible amber patina.

03 — Our approach

Objects with a sense of place.

AUREN was founded upon a quiet conviction: the objects that inhabit our domestic sanctum should command stillness rather than noise. Every dimension is derived from classical architectural proportions, allowing each piece to settle naturally within contemporary or historic volumes.

We refuse the accelerated cycle of seasonal trends. By combining precision multi-axis joinery with hand-rubbed oil finishes, our furniture is built for continuous daily life and intergenerational inheritance.
