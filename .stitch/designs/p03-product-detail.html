American Black Walnut (Juglans nigra): Sourced exclusively from managed regeneratively harvested timberlands in Pennsylvania. Dried in kiln over 40 days to a 7% moisture equilibrium to guarantee structural permanence.

Leather: 2.4mm thick full-grain bovine hides from Tärnsjö Garveri (Sweden), tanned using exclusively organic mimosa bark extracts without synthetic chromium salts.

Each chair ships individually in a custom reusable pine crate. Our white-glove partners handle room-of-choice placement, unboxing, structural inspection, and full packaging debris removal.

For international destinations outside continental North America and the EU, our logistics concierge will coordinate air freight customs clearances.

Treated with two coats of cold-pressed Danish linseed and beeswax. Re-oil every 18 months using the included Auren Maintenance Balm to enhance grain depth.

Vegetable-tanned leather will naturally acquire an amber, honeyed patina over decades, documenting the intimate life of your dwelling.

Tectonic Integrity

The Anatomy of Permanence

Every intersection in the Auren frame is calculated to resist shear stress through physical geometry rather than chemical bonds.

01 // Structural Joinery

Interlocking Mortise & Tenon

Hand-cut tenons pass entirely through the structural legs, secured with tapered walnut wedges. As seasons expand and contract the wood fiber, the joint tightens autonomously.

02 // Natural Tannins

Bark Matured Leather

Hides are immersed for 180 days in continuous baths of ground mimosa bark and chestnut extract. The resulting leather is breathable, fragrant, and hypoallergenic.

03 // Hand-Rubbed Patina

Friction Wax Lustre

Zero polyurethane varnishes. We seal the timber pores with five successive applications of raw linseed oil, buffed by hand with dried wool rags to yield a silken, natural touch.
