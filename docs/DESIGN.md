# Auren Architectural Design System

## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end architectural furniture design, grounding itself in Nordic and Mediterranean quiet luxury. The aesthetic evokes the atmosphere of a serene, stone-and-timber gallery: deliberate, permanent, tactile, and uncompromisingly refined. It addresses an audience of architects, interior designers, collectors, and design connoisseurs who prize provenance, honest materials, and spatial harmony over transient trends.

The design movement is an understated synthesis of **Architectural Minimalism** and **Editorial Craft**. It replaces synthetic digital treatments with cues from structural joinery and print publishing:
- Generous, gallery-calibrated whitespace allowing photography of physical objects to breathe.
- High dynamic typographic contrast pairing traditional editorial display serifs with ultra-tracked, micro-scale geometric sans-serif indices.
- A grounded material palette anchored in warm plaster, unlacquered bronze, and dense walnut wood tones, avoiding clinical pure whites (`#FFFFFF`) or artificial pitch blacks (`#000000`).
- Planar spatial organization: depth is defined strictly through tectonic color-blocking and inverted surface planes rather than drop shadows.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated around raw architectural materials: travertine, lime-wash plaster, hand-rubbed walnut, oiled bronze, and espresso-stained oak.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#271E18` / Dark Espresso)**: Represents structure and substance. Used for dominant typographic copy, active UI controls, inverted header/footer surfaces, and crisp architectural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#503625` / Deep Walnut)**: Evokes natural timber. Used for full-width architectural content sections (such as materials and joinery panels), slide-out navigation canvases, and contextual focus zones.
- **Tertiary (`#A7824B` / Warm Brass)**: Expresses metallic joinery and hardware. Used for deliberate textual selections (`::selection`), active focus halos, and rare highlight details.
- **Neutral (`#F7F4EE` / Warm Plaster Ivory)**: The global atmospheric canvas. Replaces clinical white with a calm, tactile, reflective background tone across viewports.

### Transparency & Opacity Rules
Light elements overlaid on deep walnut or dark photography follow strict tonal opacity tiers using the primary light tone (`#FAF8F3`):
- `82%` for primary inverted body text.
- `65%` for secondary text and descriptive subtext.
- `50%` for editorial indexing and tabular numerals.
- `15%` to `18%` for delicate architectural hairline dividers.

## Typography

Typography relies on a deliberate juxtaposition between classic literary serif proportions and precise architectural metadata.

### The Type Hierarchy
- **Display Headlines (EB Garamond)**: Set with delicate, proportional tracking and compressed line heights (`0.96` to `1.05`). Headlines must remain light (`fontWeight: 400` to `500`), commanding attention through scale rather than synthetic boldness. Mobile variants scale via fluid bounds down to readable thresholds.
- **Narrative Copy (Manrope Regular, 13px–14px)**: Generously spaced (`line-height: 1.75–1.85`) to ensure effortless reading across wide architectural panels.
- **System Labels, Navigation, and Badges (Manrope Semibold, 10px–11px)**: All utility text, buttons, column headers, and spec labels are rendered in all-caps with generous letter spacing (`0.14em` to `0.2em`), imparting the feel of technical architectural drawings.
- **Monogram & Brand Signatures**: Always set in display caps with wide `0.18em` kerning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric 12-column architectural grid contained within an absolute maximum width of `1600px`. Spacing is monumental, providing ample breathing room for sculptural furniture silhouettes.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Desktop ($\ge 1024\text{px}$)**: 12-column fluid grid. Column gap is fixed at `1.5rem` (24px). Horizontal outer margin scales dynamically from `2rem` to `3rem`. Vertical section spacing runs between `6rem` and `9rem` (`clamp(6rem, 10vw, 9rem)`).
- **Tablet ($768\text{px} - 1023\text{px}$)**: 6-column grid. Column gap drops to `1rem`. Top navigation collapses to an architectural hamburger overlay drawer. Large dual-pane splits collapse to vertical stacks.
- **Mobile ($\le 767\text{px}$)**: Single-column flow (`1fr`) with `3.5rem` vertical spacing between discrete product and editorial units. Outer canvas padding is `1.25rem`.

### Dimensional Rhythm
- Standard layout uses strict architectural aspect ratios for visual imagery: `1:1` (square), `4:5` (lounge chairs and vertical elements), `3:4` (cabinets and articles), `4:3` (workshop landscapes), and `16:9` / `16:10` (credenzas and platform beds).
- Component spacing respects a 4px/8px modular sub-grid for internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects synthetic drop shadows, skeuomorphic bevels, and artificial elevation blurs. Instead, depth is articulated through **tectonic material layering** and **spatial contrast**:

- **Planar Transitions**: Elevation is communicated by switching the ground surface color. Sections shift between plaster ivory (`#F7F4EE`), muted workshop tint (`#EAE4D9`), deep walnut (`#503625`), and dark espresso (`#271E18`).
- **Architectural Joinery Borders**: Boundaries are articulated via 1px crisp hairline dividers (`#D2C8BA` on light surfaces; `rgba(250, 248, 243, 0.16)` on dark walnut surfaces).
- **Backdrop Frosted Translucency**: The only acceptable optical blur is a restrained `16px` frosted glass treatment on the fixed header upon page scroll (`background: rgba(247, 244, 238, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), allowing imagery beneath to pass through without compromising navigation legibility.
- **Media Tonal Overlays**: Photography in full-bleed heroes and showroom panels is grounded using an ambient espresso scrim (`rgba(25, 20, 16, 0.46)`) to secure high contrast for overlying typography.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses precise structural craft:
- The fundamental corner radius is strictly `0.125rem` (2px). It is reserved exclusively for interactive button surfaces and quiet image frames, softening the digital sharpness just enough to reference hand-sanded timber corners.
- Structural cards, editorial panels, full-width sections, form inputs, modal dialogs, and dividers maintain hard `0px` rectilinear corners (`rounded-none`).
- Circles and pill shapes are strictly prohibited across buttons, tags, badges, and containers to preserve architectural authority.
